## Scope boundary

This acceptance establishes bounded support for the demonstrated standard four-string bass profile across native TuxGuitar 1.0, 1.1, 1.2, 1.3, 1.5, and modern 2.0.0.

It does not claim arbitrary compatibility with every TuxGuitar bass file.

Unless separately proven, this record does not establish support for:

- native `.tg` 0.7, 0.8, or 0.9;
- a nonexistent or inferred 1.4 route;
- five-string, six-string, or other extended-range bass profiles;
- alternate bass tunings outside the accepted standard four-string profile;
- arbitrary multi-track or multi-voice files;
- broader effects, lyrics, automation, notation, repeats, tempo structures, or other TuxGuitar features outside the accepted evidence.

Unsupported structures must fail explicitly rather than be guessed.

The previously accepted TuxGuitar six-string guitar checkpoint remains independently valid and did not require repetition for this bass acceptance.
