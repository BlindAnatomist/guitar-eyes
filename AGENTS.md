## Accessibility and semantic authority

The semantic tablature document is the authoritative representation of uploaded music. Desktop and iPhone readers must consume the same parsed blocks, measures, synchronized positions, string identities, notation states, warnings, and source-preservation records.

Do not reintroduce a separate raw-character parser for either presentation. A device-specific interface may differ in controls and spatial presentation, but it must not create a second interpretation of the music.

Preserve the real-iPhone native Files-picker focus repair and exact accepted evidence. Automated DOM tests are necessary but do not replace bounded real-device VoiceOver acceptance.
